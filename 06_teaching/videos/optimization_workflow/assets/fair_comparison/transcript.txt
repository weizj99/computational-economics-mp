Warm up, then start the clock
Warm up compilation and solver paths before measuring. Start the clock before the whole solve, and stop it once the results are returned.

Keep all three repetitions
Run three clean repetitions for each configuration and keep every timing. These bars show saved timings of the whole workload, measured without the profiler.

Report the comparison clearly
Report all three trials and their median, with the inputs, the hardware and what the clock included, so others can repeat the comparison.
