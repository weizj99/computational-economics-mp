The model behind the computation

Start with demand, competition and entry. The course then connects these choices to an economy, its estimated parameters and its implementation.

The economic model used in this course is based on De Loecker, Eeckhout and Mongey (2026), Quantifying Market Power and Business Dynamism in the Macroeconomy.

These are my independent teaching notes. They rebuild the model in Python and estimate it on the paper’s data. The course uses its own parameter values, so its results can differ from the paper’s. The notes do not represent the views of the paper’s authors. All errors are my own.
